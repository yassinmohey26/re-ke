## What this does

The requests temporarily hide these unrelated results, generally for about six months. Keeping the removed pages at 404/410 lets Google process their permanent removal. This is search-result cleanup; it does not guarantee a ranking increase or first-page placement.

No homepage, tour, destination, legitimate article, or query-parameter URL is included in this batch. No root-domain prefix block is proposed.

## Other findings to investigate separately

- 13 indexed URLs use numeric query parameters. Three use WordPress-style ?p= IDs and are not classified as spam from the URL alone.
- Ten use ?x=, ?j=, ?g=, or ?v=. Two have confirmed unrelated shopping snippets from the earlier Google search; the remaining eight need snippet/content evidence before removal.
- Search Console also contains old WordPress booking records and template URLs. They are outside this spam batch.
